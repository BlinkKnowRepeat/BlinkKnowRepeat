US tightens AI chip rules, and the limits now follow chips overseas

A US Commerce rule dated 16 November adds limits on how advanced AI chips can be used, and accessed remotely, once they are outside the United States.

प्रकाशित भेल। 2026-11-22 10:00 UTC · 4 न्यूनतम · तृतीय पक्ष द्वारा रिपोर्ट कयल गेल

मशीनी अनुवादित संस्करण

पलक मे

  • A Bureau of Industry and Security interim final rule dated 16 November 2026 tightens controls on advanced AI chips.[1]
  • It covers where chips are used and who can reach them remotely, not only where they are shipped.[1]
  • It replaces the AI Diffusion Rule’s country tiers with controls based on the destination.[1],[3]

ई किएक महत्वपूर्ण अछि?

Chips are the physical limit on how much AI anyone can build. A government that controls who may use them, and from where, shapes who can compete.

What the rule does

Law-firm summaries say the rule adds use and remote-access limits for advanced chips located outside the United States, on top of the existing export and re-export controls.[1]

Two ways to get a licence

The rule creates two licensing pathways for covered chips: one based on the destination, and one based on remote use through a data centre. It replaces the earlier AI Diffusion Rule’s worldwide tier system with destination-based controls.[1],[3]

Why remote access matters

A chip can be used without being shipped. A company can rent computing power in a data centre abroad, and the hardware never crosses another border. A rule on remote access is meant to close that route. This is our plain-language reading of the summaries, not a quotation from the rule.

How industry responded

The Semiconductor Industry Association said it looks forward to working with the administration on controls that are straightforward and effective.[2]

जकर हम पुष्टि नहि कऽ सकलहुँ। We read law-firm and trade-group summaries, not the rule text. Sources differ on the effective date: one says on publication in the Federal Register, which a public-inspection notice scheduled for 19 November 2026. Check the Federal Register text before relying on any provision.

Controls now follow the chip after it has been exported.

अहाँक कुकी विकल्पसभ

आवश्यक कुकीज साइटकेँ काज करैत रखैत अछि आ अहाँक कुकी विकल्पकेँ याद रखैत अछि। अहाँक अनुमतिसँ हम अहाँक भाषा, विषयवस्तु आ पठन सूचीकेँ सुरक्षित कऽ सकैत छी, वा यूट्यूबसँ वीडियो लोड कऽ सकैत छी। हम वर्तमानमे कोनो विज्ञापन वा विश्लेषण कुकीजक उपयोग नहि करैत छी।

अपन विकल्पकेँ कोनो समय बदलि लिअ। कुकीजकेँ प्रबंधित करू पादलेख मे। हमर सभ केँ देखू। गोपनीयता नीति.

कुकी सेटिंगसभ

अहाँ जे अनुमति देबय चाहैत छी, से चुनू। अहाँ एहि सेटिंगसभकेँ कोनो समय बदल सकैत छी।

एहि उपकरण पर कथाकेँ सेव करबाक लेल वरीयता सभक अनुमति दियौक।

बाहरी मीडियाकेँ यूट्यूब प्लेयरकेँ लोड करबाक अनुमति दियौक।

आवश्यकअपन कुकी विकल्पकेँ याद राखू आ जखन अहाँ एकर उपयोग करैत छी तँ साइन-इनक समर्थन करू।सदा चालू।
