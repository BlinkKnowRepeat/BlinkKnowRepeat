आमचो नदर

Our name is our method: a quick look first, then the knowledge behind it, then a habit you can repeat.

  1. 1

    Blink

    Get the story in a moment. Every story opens with “In a blink”: three short points you can read in under a minute. The slideshow and the homepage show the newest news first, so you can scan the day quickly.

  2. 2

    Know

    Understand why it matters and how we know. Each story explains why it matters, then shows the evidence. Sources are numbered, claims point to them, and a label tells you whether we used a primary source or other people’s reporting. We also say what we could not confirm.

  3. 3

    Repeat

    Make it a habit. Come back for new stories in each section, save the ones you want to return to, or follow along by RSS. When something changes or we get it wrong, we publish a correction.

गरजेच्यो कुकीज साइट काम करत रावतात आनी तुमची कुकी निवडी याद दवरतात. तुमच्या परवानगीन आमी तुमची भास, विशय आनी वाचन वळेरी सांबाळून दवरूंक शकतात, वा युट्युबा वयल्यान व्हिडियो लोड करूंक शकतात. आमी सद्या जायरात वा विश्लेशण कुकीज वापरनात.
