What happens when you tap send?

Follow one tiny packet from your phone, through your router, and out into the internet. Open the full interactive version in English, Hindi, Gujarati or Marathi.

Published Nov 22, 2026, 08:00 UTC · 4 min · Concept explainer

In a blink

  • Your message is split into packets that carry data plus delivery information.
  • Wi-Fi is only the first hop: it ends at your access point.
  • Routers pass the packet along; each router only decides the next step.

Why it matters

Knowing where the wireless link ends helps you tell a home Wi-Fi problem from a problem farther along the internet route.

Your phone prepares the data

The app turns what you typed into data. Network software packages it into packets, each carrying part of the data plus the addressing information routers use to move it toward the destination.

Wi-Fi is the first hop

Your phone uses radio waves to reach the access point. In many homes the access point and router share one box. The wireless hop ends there; the router then forwards the packet toward your internet provider.

The network takes it from there

Routers forward packets hop by hop. This is a simplified picture: it leaves out encryption, retransmission and the details that differ between apps.
