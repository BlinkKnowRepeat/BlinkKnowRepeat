Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the operator of Blink, Know, Repeat (“we”) collects when you use Blink, Know, Repeat and why. We collect as little as we can.

What stays on your device

  • If you allow Preferences, your theme and bookmarks are saved in your browser’s local storage. We do not receive them.
  • If you allow Preferences, your language is saved in a first-party cookie (bkr_locale) for up to 180 days. A separate cookie remembers your consent choices for up to 180 days.

What we receive

  • Daily Insights email. If you subscribe, we store your email address, language and the time you agreed. We use it only to send the newsletter once email delivery is switched on. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • Google sign-in (optional, when enabled). We receive your name, email address and profile identifier from Google to create your account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive or contacts.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes IP addresses and request details to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

What we do not do

We do not run advertising or analytics trackers at present, and we do not sell your personal data. If that changes, this page and the Cookies page will be updated first and a preference control will be provided where required.

Why we use data

To deliver the site, remember your choices, send the newsletter you asked for, keep the service secure, and meet legal duties.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that run the site for us (hosting and, when enabled, email delivery) and where the law requires it. Cloudflare may process data in countries other than yours.

Links to other sites

When you click a source link, that site’s own privacy practices apply. If you allow External media and choose to load a YouTube video, your browser connects to YouTube. YouTube can then receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details.

Retention and your rights

We keep newsletter records until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them, and account data until you ask us to delete your account. You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data. Use the Contact page. You may also have rights under laws such as India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes

We will post changes here with a new date. Last updated: 23 November 2026.
