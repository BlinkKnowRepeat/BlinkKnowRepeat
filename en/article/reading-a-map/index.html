What does a map projection change?

Compare what a flat map can preserve when it depicts a round Earth.

Nov 22, 2026, 08:00 UTC · 5 min read · Original article · AI-assisted

Beginner

Why it matters

A projection suitable for comparing areas can look very different from a projection chosen for navigation.

Flatten a curved surface

Draw a grid on an orange peel and try to lay it flat. The gaps and stretching show why any flat representation must distort some properties of the sphere.

Ask what the map preserves

An equal-area projection preserves area ratios. Other projections preserve local angles or particular distances. Read the legend and map title before measuring or comparing.
