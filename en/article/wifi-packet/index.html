What happens when you tap send?

Follow one tiny packet from your phone, through your router, and out into the internet.

Nov 22, 2026, 08:00 UTC · 4 min read · Original article · AI-assisted

Why it matters

Knowing where the wireless link ends helps you distinguish a home Wi-Fi problem from a problem farther along the internet route.

Follow the packet

Your phone prepares the data

When you send a message, the app turns what you typed into data. Network software packages data for delivery. A packet carries part of that data alongside information the network uses to move it toward its destination.

Wi-Fi is the first hop

Your phone uses radio waves to reach your Wi-Fi access point. In many homes, the access point and router share one box. The wireless hop ends at the access point, while the router forwards the packet toward your internet provider.

The network takes it from there

Routers forward packets along the network. A message may go to a service before the recipient receives it. The diagram simplifies the trip and leaves out encryption, retransmissions, and other details that vary by app.
