Discovery+ is getting dearer: what the price rise tells you

The US price rise was announced on 13 October, days after Skydance completed its takeover of the service’s parent. Existing customers see it on bills from 12 November.

प्रकाशित 22 नव॰ 2026, 13:00 UTC · 3 न्यूनतम · तीसरे पक्ष द्वारा रिपोर्ट किया गया

मशीन-अनुवादित संस्करण

एक झलक में

  • The ad-supported plan rises by $1 to $6.99 a month, and the ad-free plan by $2 to $11.99.[1]
  • Existing customers got 30 days’ notice, with new charges on bills from 12 November 2026.[1]
  • The rise came just days after Skydance completed its acquisition of the service’s parent operations.[1],[2]

यह क्यों मायने रखता है

A price rise soon after a takeover is a common pattern: new owners look for revenue they can raise quickly. Knowing that helps you decide whether to keep paying.

संख्याएँ

The ad-supported plan goes from $5.99 to $6.99, and the ad-free plan from $9.99 to $11.99, in the United States.[1]

The timing

The change was announced on 13 October 2026, days after Skydance completed its acquisition. Existing subscribers have 30 days’ notice, so the new charge shows on bills from 12 November.[1],[2]

Decoding the wording

Companies often describe such changes as an adjustment to reflect value. Plainly, it means you pay more. This is our reading of the wording, not a statement from the company.

What you can do

Check your next bill, compare the ad-supported plan with the ad-free one, and cancel or pause if you watch it rarely. Most services let you rejoin later.

जिन बातों की पुष्टि अभी नहीं हुई Prices are for the United States. We have not confirmed the previous prices with the company; they follow from the reported increases.

आपके कुकी विकल्प

आवश्यक कुकीज़ साइट को काम करती रहती हैं और आपके कुकी विकल्पों को याद रखती हैं। आपकी अनुमति से, हम आपकी भाषा, विषय और पढ़ने की सूची को सहेजा जा सकता है, या यूट्यूब से वीडियो लोड कर सकते हैं। हम वर्तमान में कोई विज्ञापन या विश्लेषण कुकीज़ का उपयोग नहीं करते हैं।

किसी भी समय अपनी पसंद बदलें कुकीज़ प्रबंधित करें पादलेख में। हमारा देखें गोपनीयता नीति.
