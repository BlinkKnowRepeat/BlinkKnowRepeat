What “all-stock” means in the Equitable and Corebridge merger

The two insurers agreed an all-stock merger that is expected to close by the end of 2026. Corebridge’s chief executive is slated to lead the combined company.

प्रकाशित 22 नव॰ 2026, 16:00 UTC · 3 न्यूनतम · तीसरे पक्ष द्वारा रिपोर्ट किया गया

मशीन-अनुवादित संस्करण

एक झलक में

  • Equitable and Corebridge agreed an all-stock merger.[1],[2]
  • It is expected to close by the end of 2026.[2]
  • Corebridge CEO Marc Costantini is slated to become CEO of the new company.[2]

यह क्यों मायने रखता है

How a deal is paid for tells you who carries the risk. In an all-stock deal, shareholders receive shares, not cash, so their value moves with the new company’s share price.

The deal in short

Equitable and Corebridge announced an all-stock merger. Shares of both companies rose after the surprise announcement.[1]

What “all-stock” means

Instead of paying cash, the buyer pays with its own shares. Neither company spends cash, but shareholders’ final value depends on how the combined company’s shares trade. This is a general explanation, not specific to these terms.

Who leads

Corebridge CEO Marc Costantini is slated to become CEO of the new company, and the deal is expected to close by the end of 2026.[2]

क्या देखना है

Closing needs the usual approvals. Until then, the companies run separately.

जिन बातों की पुष्टि अभी नहीं हुई We have not read the merger agreement. The exchange ratio and other terms are not covered here.

आपके कुकी विकल्प

आवश्यक कुकीज़ साइट को काम करती रहती हैं और आपके कुकी विकल्पों को याद रखती हैं। आपकी अनुमति से, हम आपकी भाषा, विषय और पढ़ने की सूची को सहेजा जा सकता है, या यूट्यूब से वीडियो लोड कर सकते हैं। हम वर्तमान में कोई विज्ञापन या विश्लेषण कुकीज़ का उपयोग नहीं करते हैं।

किसी भी समय अपनी पसंद बदलें कुकीज़ प्रबंधित करें पादलेख में। हमारा देखें गोपनीयता नीति.
