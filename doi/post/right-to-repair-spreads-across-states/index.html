Right to repair is spreading: 13 US states now have a law

A July 2026 legal analysis counts 13 states with a right-to-repair statute. Texas’s law took effect on 1 September, and a farm-equipment case was settled.

प्रकाशित। 2026-11-22 19:00 UTC · 4 मिन · त्रिया पक्षें द्वारा रिपोर्ट कीता गेआ

मशीन-अनुवादत संस्करण

इक पलक च

  • As of July 2026, 13 US states had enacted some form of right-to-repair law.[1]
  • Texas’s law applies to electronics sold from 1 September 2026 that cost at least $50 wholesale.[1],[2]
  • The FTC’s case against Deere & Company was settled in July 2026, with Deere agreeing to open its repair ecosystem.[1]

एह् की म्हत्तवपूर्ण ऐ?

If you can get parts, manuals and tools, a broken phone or tractor can be fixed instead of replaced. That affects what you spend and how long things last.

The state picture

A legal analysis dated 30 July 2026 counts 13 states with some form of right-to-repair statute.[1] PIRG reports 57 active bills in 22 states so far in 2026.[3]

Electronics

Texas’s law applies from 1 September 2026 to electronic devices and components sold to consumers that cost at least $50 wholesale. Connecticut’s took effect on 1 July 2026.[1],[2]

Parts pairing

Washington requires manufacturers to stop using parts pairing to block repairs from 1 January 2026. Parts pairing is software that refuses a part unless the maker approves it.[1]

Farm equipment

The FTC’s monopolisation case against Deere & Company settled in July 2026. Deere agreed to open its repair ecosystem to farmers and independent technicians.[1]

जिंʼदी असीं पुश्टी नेईं करी सके। These laws are US state laws and do not apply in India. Details and exemptions vary by state.

तुंʼदी कुकी विकल्प

ज़रूरी कुकीज़ साइट गी कम्म करदे रखदे न ते तुंʼदे कुकी विकल्पें गी चेता रखदे न। तुंʼदी इजाज़त कन्नै, असीं तुंʼदी भाशा, थीम ते पढ़ने दी सूची गी सहेजी सकदे न, जां यूट्यूब थमां वीडियो लोड करी सकदे न। असीं वर्तमान च कोई विज्ञापन जां विश्लेषण कुकीज़ दा इस्तेमाल नेईं करदे।

अपने विकल्पें गी कुसै बी समें बदलो कुकीज़ दा प्रबंधन करो फुटर च। हमारा दस्सो निजता नीति.

कुकी सेटिंग

चुनो जे तुस केह् मंजूर करना चाह्न्नां। तुस इʼनें सेटिंग्स गी कुसै बी समें बदली सकदे ओ।

इस उपकरण पर कहानियें गी सहेजने आस्तै वरीयताएं गी इजाज़त देओ।
